ATS CV on iPhone: make your CV readable before you upload it

Most online applications pass through an applicant tracking system (ATS) before a recruiter opens them. If the system cannot read your CV, the experience you worked hard to describe can be split, skipped or filed under the wrong heading. Here is what makes a CV ATS-friendly, and how to check yours on your iPhone with a 0–100 ATS score before you send it.

What makes a CV ATS-friendly

  1. One column, top to bottom. Two-column designs can be read across the columns, mixing lines from both sides.
  2. Standard headings. Profile or Summary, Experience, Education, Skills, Certifications. Creative labels make it harder for the system to sort your sections.
  3. Keywords from the job post, spelled the same way. Job titles, tools and skills the employer lists should appear in your own CV text.
  4. Real, selectable text. A scanned page or text inside an image gives the system nothing to read.

Not sure whether the role wants a CV or a resume? See Resume vs CV: differences in the US, UK, Europe and academia.

Tailor one CV per application

Sending the same file everywhere is the fastest way to miss keywords. In CV Desk you can keep multiple CVs and tailor one for each job, so a version for an operations role and a version for a project role sit side by side. Live preview shows the finished layout as you type, and clean templates with designer accent colors keep it recruiter-ready.

Score it, fix it, then export

  1. Check the 0–100 ATS score. It appears instantly from what you have entered.
  2. Tap the fixes. Clear, tappable suggestions show what to change, and you can watch the score move.
  3. Export the right file. A single-column PDF with real, selectable text and standard headings for upload forms, or an editable Word (.docx) file when a recruiter asks for one. Share it, save it to Files, or AirPrint it.

Your CV stays on your iPhone, with no account and no upload, and it works in airplane mode. Siri & Shortcuts let you jump straight back in.

Frequently asked questions

Is an ATS CV different from an ATS resume?

The name changes with the region and the field: UK, European and many international postings say CV, while most US postings say resume, and academic CVs run longer. The rules an applicant tracking system cares about are the same for both: readable layout, standard headings and the right keywords. Use the word the job post uses.

Should I put a photo on my CV?

Follow the employer's instructions. An ATS does not read photos. In CV Desk the header photo is optional and off by default, which keeps the layout ATS-safe.

Can I edit my CV in Word after I build it?

Yes. Export an editable .docx and open it in Word, Pages or Google Docs with the same fonts, colors and layout.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything happens on your iPhone, your CV never leaves the device, and the app works in airplane mode.

Will a high ATS score get me an interview?

No score can promise that. The ATS Score is an on-device estimate to improve parse-safety and keyword coverage; results vary by employer system.
