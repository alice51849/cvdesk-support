ATS resume checker on iPhone: check it before you hit Apply

You found the job, you tailored the resume, and it is late. Before you upload, it is worth one quick check: can an applicant tracking system (ATS) actually read what you wrote?

What an ATS resume check looks for

Why does an ATS check matter?

Many employers run applications through an applicant tracking system before a person reads them. If the system cannot parse your layout, headings or keywords, your experience can be read incorrectly. A check before you apply catches the problems you can fix in a few minutes.

What should I fix first?

Start with the format: a single-column layout with standard headings (Experience, Education, Skills) and real, selectable text rather than text inside images. Then check keyword coverage against the job post, so the skills the employer asks for appear in your own words. Keep a separate tailored version for each job instead of one generic file.

How does CV Desk check a resume?

CV Desk gives your resume an instant 0–100 ATS score on your iPhone, with clear, tappable fixes. It then exports a single-column PDF with real, selectable text and standard headings, or an editable Word (.docx) file you can open in Word, Pages or Google Docs. The ATS score is an on-device estimate to improve parse-safety and keyword coverage; results vary by employer system.

Do I have to pay or create an account?

No account is needed. You can build and preview your resume without a purchase; a single one-time unlock in the app lets you export with no watermark. There is no subscription and there are no ads. You can see the price on the App Store page before you buy. CV Desk is also on Google Play for Android.

Is my resume private?

Your resume stays on your iPhone and the app works in airplane mode. The optional header photo is off by default, which keeps the layout ATS-safe.
