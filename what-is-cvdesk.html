What is CV Desk?

CV Desk is an iPhone app that builds resumes made to be read by applicant tracking systems (ATS). You write your resume with a live preview, get an instant 0–100 ATS score with tappable fixes, then export a single-column PDF with real, selectable text or an editable Word (.docx) file. The ATS score is an on-device estimate of how safely your resume parses and how well it covers keywords; results vary by employer system.

Who is CV Desk for?

Job seekers who apply through online forms and want their experience and keywords read correctly, people who keep a different resume for each job, and anyone who prefers that a resume never leaves their phone. It works in airplane mode and needs no account.

How does CV Desk charge?

Building a resume and previewing it needs no purchase. A one-time unlock removes the watermark when you export. The listing states there is no subscription, no ads and no account. For the current price, check the App Store page, because prices differ by country.

How is CV Desk different from other resume apps?

Based on what the listing describes: it scores the resume before you apply and points to specific fixes; its PDF is one column with real selectable text and standard headings; you can export an editable .docx and open it in Word, Pages or Google Docs; you can keep several tailored resumes; the optional header photo is off by default; and the whole thing runs on the iPhone with no account.
