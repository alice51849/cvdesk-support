ATS CV builder on iPhone: write a UK CV that screening software can read

Many UK job sites and employers run applications through an applicant tracking system (ATS) before a recruiter reads them. CV Desk is a CV builder for iPhone that keeps your layout ATS-safe while you write, scores your CV from 0 to 100, and exports a PDF or an editable Word file when you are ready to apply.

Build the CV in a layout an ATS can read

  1. Start with a personal profile. Two or three lines on who you are and the role you want, under a plain “Profile” heading.
  2. Use standard headings. Profile, Experience, Education, Skills, Certifications. Clear labels help the system file each section correctly.
  3. Keep it to one column. The exported PDF is single-column, so lines are read top to bottom rather than across two sides.
  4. Write in real text. The exported PDF keeps real, selectable text.

Tailor one CV per application

Copy the job title, tools and skills from the advert into your own wording, spelled the same way. CV Desk lets you keep multiple CVs, so a version for each role sits side by side instead of one file sent everywhere. CV Desk focuses on clean, professional templates.

Check the ATS score, fix it, then export

  1. See the 0–100 ATS score. It updates instantly on your iPhone from what you have entered.
  2. Tap the suggested fixes. Each suggestion shows what to change, and you can watch the score move.
  3. Export the right file. A single-column PDF for online forms, or an editable Word (.docx) file when an agency asks for one.

You can create and preview your CV without a purchase; a one-time unlock exports it without a watermark, with no subscription. No account, no upload, and it works in airplane mode.

Frequently asked questions

What should a UK CV include?

Usually your name and contact details, a short personal profile, work experience in reverse chronological order, education, and skills. Follow the job advert if it asks for something different. CV Desk uses standard headings so an applicant tracking system can sort each section.

Should I add a photo to a UK CV?

Photos are generally left off UK CVs unless the employer asks for one, and an ATS does not read photos.

Can I send an editable Word CV to a recruitment agency?

Yes. Export an editable .docx, or export a single-column PDF with real, selectable text for online application forms.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. Your CV stays on your iPhone, nothing is uploaded, and the app works in airplane mode.

Will a high ATS score guarantee an interview?

No. The ATS score is an on-device estimate that helps with readability and keyword coverage; results vary by employer system.
